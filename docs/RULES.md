# Supersonic overland rules by country

Checked 11 Oct 2026. Machine-readable copy: `site/geo/rules.json`. Rows marked unverified need the national AIP (GEN 1.7, ENR 1.1).

| Code | Country | Status | What the rule tests | Instrument | Pending | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | United States | ban; standard proposed | Mach number today; proposed: boom at the surface ≤ 0.11 psf (primary + secondary) | 14 CFR 91.817/91.818; NPRM FAA-2026-6935 (2 Jul 2026) | Final rule targeted mid-2027; SAM Act passed House, cleared Senate Commerce | [link](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/02/2026-13440/enabling-supersonic-overland-flight) |
| DE | Germany | permit | Boom not detectable on the ground (exceptions to the EU VFR ban) | § 38 LuftVO | none found | [link](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/luftvo_2015/__38.html) |
| NZ | New Zealand | permit | Above M0.92 needs approval; boom must not reach the surface on flights entering or leaving NZ | CAR Part 91, rule 91.805 | none found | [link](https://www.aviation.govt.nz/rules/rule-part/part-91/subpart-j/) |
| CA | Canada | ban | Mach number (true Mach ≥ 1) | CARs SOR/96-433, Supersonic Flight | none found | [link](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433/page-57.html) |
| EU | EU / EASA (incl. FR, NO; CH assumed) | permit (VFR only) | Mach number; no EU rule for IFR | Reg (EU) 923/2012 SERA.5005(d)(1) | NPA 2026-02 covers take-off/landing noise only | [link](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/1185/annexes/2016-07-20/data.xht) |
| GB | United Kingdom | permit (VFR); IFR gap | Mach number | UK SERA.5005(d)(1) | Proposed SERA.5015(d) for IFR (CAA Task 0170, 2024); adoption unverified | [link](https://www.caa.co.uk/media/mtbjmxer/task_0170_prohibition_of_supersonic_flight_over_land_oid.pdf) |
| RU | Russia | permit | Mach number, allowed above 11,000 m or in special zones | Government Decree No. 138 (2010), para 50 | none found | [link](https://pravo.ppt.ru/postanovlenie/pravitelstvo/n-138-35389) |
| AU | Australia | permit | Supersonic-capable aircraft need an approval with conditions | Air Navigation (Aircraft Noise) Regulations 2018, s 6(2), s 16 | none found | [link](https://legislation.gov.au/F2018L00448/asmade/2018-03-29/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) |
| BR | Brazil | permit | Mach number (authorisation to exceed Mach 1) | RBAC 91 §91.1713 (Amdt 07, 2026) | none found | [link](https://pergamum.anac.gov.br/pergamum/vinculos/RBAC91EMD07.pdf) |

**Instrument not found (general ban assumed):** MX, AR, CL, CO, CN, JP, IN, KR, SG, AE, SA, QA, TR, EG, ZA, KE.

**ICAO:** No en-route standard; CAEP/14 drafting, not expected before about 2031. Annex 2: VFR supersonic only with ATS authorisation. Chicago Convention Art. 1-2: sovereignty over land and the 12 nm territorial sea.

**Why it matters:** only the US proposal, Germany and New Zealand write the rule about the boom reaching the ground. Everywhere else the test is the aircraft's Mach number, so Mach cutoff does not unlock speed there until the rule changes. The map treats every territory as restricted until its rule says otherwise.
